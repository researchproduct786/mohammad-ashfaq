        The Water Cooling Experiment

        From a simple problem to an engineered solution.

        June ki garmi mein ek simple problem thi: normal/warm water ko cool kaise kiya jaye?

        During a hot June at school there was no proper cold-water system. Sir and the students experimented with a way to take normal water and cool it. It was a hands-on school experiment, not a factory machine, and it is shown here as exactly that.
